<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As** Jason, the analyst behind a public portfolio app, **I want to** run one job that turns every detected SPY episode since 1993 into a sourced, schema-validated news explanation (or an honest `unexplained` / `needs_review`), within a hard $25 budget, and then review and override the results locally, **so that** the Event Explorer in Phase 4 can show explanations that are traceable and never presented as fact without a dated source.

This slice delivers the review half end to end: a local CLI reads events, lets Jason decide per record, writes `data/event_overrides.json`, and a pure read-time merge (`load_effective_events`) gives Phase 4 the effective view without ever rewriting `events.json`. It runs today against NullProvider output, so it does not wait for the paid backfill.

Purpose: REV-01, REV-02 per D-05 (CLI, no Streamlit UI, overrides win at read time, never merged on disk).
Output: core/news/overrides.py, override/effective readers in core/storage.py, scripts/review_events.py, tests.
</objective>
